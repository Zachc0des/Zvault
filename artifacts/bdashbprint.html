Recovery Plan — net negative to net positive

Buy before building. Cheap tools are the bridge; the custom platform is the target, funded by profit — not debt.
PHASE 1 · MONTH 1 OF 6

The sequence

Revenue first, discipline second, capacity third. Build last.
Months 1–2 · Active now
Cash up, near-zero cost

Collections (7/14/30 reminders, deposits, tap-to-pay) · price & margin audit · missed-call text-back · review engine. Revenue up before a dollar goes to software.

Months 3–4
Spending controlled

Bucket discipline enforced against thresholds every week · monthly statement rhythm locked in · kill or reprice any line that can't cover its allocation.

Months 5–6
Capacity & scale

Partner exchange covers peak season with zero payroll commitment · ad spend scales only from margin, never from reserve.

The gate
Build BDash custom — only after 3 consecutive net-positive months

Until then this dashboard is the target architecture running on off-the-shelf rails. Profit funds the build.

Risk register — what could sink it

Named, owned, and mitigated — not hoped away.
Build scopeAccounting + OCR + telephony + payroll + CRM + ads is six industries. Mitigation: the build gate above.
GATED
Payroll, tax & classificationWithholding errors and crew rent/swap arrangements carry IRS & labor-law penalties. Mitigation: accountant + attorney review before any transfer executes.
EXTERNAL REVIEW
Single point of failureIf the dashboard dies, mail, phone, invoicing and payroll die with it. Mitigation: triple store + checkpoint restore + bridge tools stay usable standalone.
REDUNDANT
PII & financial data concentrationClient, HR and finance records in one place raise the stakes. Mitigation: encryption at rest, per-role access, tested restores — real, not aspirational.
IN PROGRESS
OCR corruption of the booksBad invoice totals silently poison the ledger. Mitigation: low-confidence scans block storage until a human clears them.
ENFORCED
AdoptionIf the crew won't change habits, the software changes nothing. Mitigation: the weekly 20-minute review is the one non-negotiable habit.
HABIT-BASED
SIMULATED DATA · PAYROLL, TAX & CONTRACT STRUCTURES REQUIRE A QUALIFIED ACCOUNTANT AND ATTORNEY BEFORE IMPLEMENTATION
